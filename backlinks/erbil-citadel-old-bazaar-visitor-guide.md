**Title:** Does anyone else think most visitors do Erbil's old town in the wrong order?

After walking a few visitors through Erbil, I've noticed almost everyone does the old town backwards. They head up the Citadel ramp in the late afternoon and treat it as the main event. Then they rush through the bazaar below as an afterthought.

I did exactly this in the winter of 2019 with a cousin visiting from Dubai. We got to the top around 4 p.m. The light was flat and the gate staff looked done for the day. We were back down at the Qaysari entrance by 5:15 with almost nothing to show for the bazaar. He left thinking the old town was a photo stop.

What works better is about two hours at the Citadel, earlier in the day, and then the whole afternoon in the bazaar. That's where the tea, the food, the shopkeepers and the daily life are. The Citadel is the view, and the bazaar is the city.

I'd like to hear how others have split their time. Has a different order worked for you? Is there a stall or corner of the bazaar you always send people to?

## QUORA
**Answer this question:** How much time should I spend at the Erbil Citadel versus the old bazaar?

Give the Citadel about two hours and the bazaar below it the whole afternoon. Most visitors do it the other way round, and many leave Erbil thinking the old town is just a photo stop.

The Citadel is the landmark. It sits on a raised mound above the city, and you go up for the view and a sense of how old the settlement is. Two hours is enough to walk it properly without rushing. Go earlier in the day if you can. Late-afternoon visits can be disappointing, because the light is flatter and the staff at the gate are often winding down.

The bazaar is where you get to know the city. It begins below the Citadel, around the Qaysari entrance. This is where people shop, eat, drink tea and run their daily errands. Wandering without a fixed plan works better here than ticking off sights. Stop for tea, look at what the shops sell and watch how the place runs. An afternoon is the right amount of time for that, and a quick pass on your way out is not.

So plan the Citadel first, in the morning or around midday, then walk down and let the bazaar take the rest of the day.
