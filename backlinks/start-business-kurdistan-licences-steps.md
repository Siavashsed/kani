## QUORA
**Answer this question:** What are the steps to start a business in the Kurdistan Region of Iraq?

Starting a business in the Kurdistan Region follows a fairly consistent sequence, and the biggest mistake newcomers make is doing the steps out of order.

1. Register the company with the KRG company registrar. This is the foundation. You will typically need your articles of association, identification for the founders, and the proposed company name and activity. Bring originals and several photocopies.

2. Get a tax number. Once the company exists on paper, you register with the tax authority so you can operate and invoice legally.

3. Join the chamber of commerce. Membership is generally expected before you can proceed to sector-specific approvals, and it also gives you access to a useful local network.

4. Collect your sector licence. Depending on what you do, whether food, retail, tourism, construction or services, you may need approval from the relevant ministry or directorate.

5. Obtain the municipal permit. This ties your business to a physical location in Erbil (Hewler), Sulaymaniyah (Slemani) or another city, and often involves an inspection of the premises.

Practical advice: go early in the morning, carry more copies than you think you need, and call each office beforehand, because requirements and forms change. Do not assume that a process in one city is identical in another. Budget extra time for stamps and signatures, and if possible work with someone who knows the local procedures or a patient local partner.
